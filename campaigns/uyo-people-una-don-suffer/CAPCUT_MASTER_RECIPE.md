# CAPCUT MASTER EDITING RECIPE CARD
## Campaign: "Uyo People, Una Don Suffer! 🥱" (50–56s | 1080 × 1920)

Follow this step-by-step guide to assemble your commercial in **CapCut PC or Mobile** in under 10 minutes without triggering any paywalls or watermarks.

---

### Phase 1: Project Canvas Setup
1. Open CapCut and click **New Project**.
2. Click **Ratio** below the preview window and select **9:16 (TikTok/Reels)**.
3. In **Settings**, ensure the project resolution is set to **1080 × 1920** at **60 fps** (or 30 fps).

---

### Phase 2: Timeline Assembly (The 7 Scenes)
Drag your 7 generated video clips from Google AI Studio / Veo onto the main video track:

| Clip | Timestamp | Action / Content | Sound Cue |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clip 1** | `00:00 - 00:08` | 3 guys talking in Uyo compound; speaker turns to camera | Percussion kick + bouncy Afrobeats start |
| **Clip 2** | `00:08 - 00:16` | Man unboxing parcel; discovers wrong item | 💥 **SFX:** Record scratch or dramatic freeze sting |
| **Clip 3** | `00:16 - 00:24` | Woman showing mismatched shoe to friend | 🤦‍♂️ **SFX:** Short comedic drum roll / rimshot |
| **Clip 4** | `00:24 - 00:32` | Man smiling, browsing Victorious Market on phone | Afrobeats transitions to bright, positive melody |
| **Clip 5** | `00:32 - 00:40` | Luxury showroom montage (Phones, fans, sneakers) | Smooth, modern commercial synth swells |

| **Clip 7** | `00:48 - 00:56` | Royal purple & gold branded card with phone & website | Brand audio sonic logo resolution |

### Phase 3: Subtitles (100% Free Without Pro Paywall)
Do **not** use the paid auto-caption tool. Instead:
1. Click **Text** in the top menu.
2. Select **Local Subtitles** and click **Import**.
3. Select your file: [`campaigns/uyo-people-una-don-suffer/scenes/subtitles.srt`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Downloads/vmcreatives/campaigns/uyo-people-una-don-suffer/scenes/subtitles.srt).
4. Select all subtitle blocks on your timeline (`Ctrl + A` on the text track) to style them simultaneously:
   * **Font:** `Bebas Neue` or `Montserrat Extra Bold` / `The Bold Font`.
   * **Font Size:** 14 – 18 (visible on mobile without covering faces).
   * **Color:** Vibrant Yellow (`#FFE600`) or Gold (`#FFD700`).
   * **Stroke:** Solid Black (`#000000`), Thickness: 25% – 35%.
   * **Position:** Y-axis: -650 (sitting cleanly in the lower third).
   * **Animation (Free):** Under *In Animation*, pick **"Bounce In"** or **"Pop Up"** (ensure no "PRO" badge).

---

### Phase 4: Audio Track Mixing (The Golden Ratios)
Place your audio tracks on separate layers and set the volumes:

```
Track 1: Nigerian Pidgin Voiceover ────► Volume: 0.0 dB (Crisp & Center)
Track 2: Comedic Sound Effects (SFX) ──► Volume: -3.0 dB to -5.0 dB
Track 3: Background Afrobeats Music ──► Volume: -16.0 dB to -18.0 dB (Ducked under voice)
```

> **Pro Tip:** In CapCut, enable **Auto Audio Ducking** on the music track so the Afrobeats automatically dips whenever the actors are speaking!

---
