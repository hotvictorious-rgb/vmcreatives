### 🛠 Production Pipeline (Google AI Studio + CapCut)
1. **Google AI Studio (Veo 3.1):** Generate clips 1 to 7 individually. Set aspect ratio to **9:16** and duration to **8 seconds**.
2. **CapCut Assembly:**
   - Import clips 1 to 7 into a 1080x1920 timeline.
   - Import `subtitles.srt` (from `campaigns/uyo-people-una-don-suffer/scenes/subtitles.srt`).
   - Style subtitles: Bold Sans-serif, Yellow/White text with Purple `#5E17EB` stroke or background pill.
   - Add comedic sound effects (record scratch, rimshot, cartoon freeze sting).
   - Overlay the official Victorious Market logo file on the top corner and final scene.
   - Export at 1080p, 60fps, high bitrate.
