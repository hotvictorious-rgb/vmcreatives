# CAPCUT MASTER FINISHING: SCENE 7 TEXT & LOGO OVERLAY GUIDE
## How to Add Your Phone Number & Website with 100% Zero Typos

Because we designed **Scene 7** as a clean 3D motion plate (with Victorious gesturing to an empty left-center area), you avoid the #1 failure of AI video generators: garbled numbers and misspelled website addresses.

Follow these 4 simple steps in CapCut to overlay your branding with crisp 4K broadcast clarity:

---

### Step 1: Import Scene 7 onto the Main Video Track
* Drag your generated 8-second Scene 7 video clip onto the timeline.
* Victorious will appear on the right side of the screen smiling and gesturing toward the empty left side.

---

### Step 2: Add the Official Victorious Market Logo
1. Click **Media → Import** and select your high-res transparent Victorious Market logo PNG.
2. Drag it onto a track **above** the video during Scene 7 (from `00:48` to `00:56`).
3. **Position:** Center-left (above Victorious’s gesture).
4. **Scale:** Adjust size to ~25%–30%.
5. **Animation:** Click *Animation → In → "Fade In"* (0.5s duration).

---

### Step 3: Add the Brand Tagline & Contact Details
Click **Text → Add Default Text** and add 3 clean text layers:

#### Layer A (Brand Title & Tagline):
* **Text:**
  ```text
  VICTORIOUS MARKET
  Your Trusted Online Market
  ```
* **Font:** `Montserrat Extra Bold` or `Bebas Neue`
* **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle Gold (`#FFD700`) outline/glow.
* **Size:** 16 – 18 pt.

#### Layer B (Phone & WhatsApp):
* **Text:** `📞 09118949035`
* **Font:** Bold Sans-serif
* **Color:** Electric Gold (`#FFD700`)
* **Size:** 20 pt (Make it large, bold, and readable on small mobile screens).

#### Layer C (Website & Location):
* **Text:**
  ```text
  🌐 shop.victoriousmarket.com.ng
  📍 8 Ekpo Obot Street, Uyo
  ```
* **Font:** Clean Sans-serif (e.g. `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Poppins`)
* **Color:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
* **Size:** 14 pt.

---

### Step 4: Add the Final Brand Audio Chime
* At `00:48`, right when the logo and phone number appear, add a bright, uplifting **corporate chime** or **sonic logo sound effect**.
* Lower the background Afrobeats music by -3dB so the final voiceover speaks with absolute clarity:
  > *"Victorious Market! Your trusted online market for quality products. Order today!"*
