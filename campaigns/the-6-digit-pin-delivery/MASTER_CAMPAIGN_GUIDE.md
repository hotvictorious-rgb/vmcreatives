In Nigeria, the #1 reason people hesitate to buy gadgets and high-ticket products online is **fear of delivery scams or delivery riders delivering counterfeit / swapped goods**. 
Victorious Market solves this with an unforgeable **6-Digit Delivery PIN Verification**:
