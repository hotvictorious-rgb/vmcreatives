### Step 3: Assemble & Export (5 Mins)

#### In CapCut (Free Desktop/Mobile):
1. Create a **9:16** project and drag in your 7 clips.
2. Click **Text → Local Subtitles → Import** and select [`subtitles.srt`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Downloads/vmcreatives/campaigns/uyo-people-una-don-suffer/scenes/subtitles.srt).
3. Style the subtitles: Yellow text (`#FFE600`), bold black stroke, font: `Montserrat` or `Bebas Neue`.
4. Add your music track (set volume to `-16 dB`).
5. Overlay your official Victorious Market logo in the top corner.
6. Click **Export** ➔ 1080p 60fps (no watermarks, zero cost!).

*(Or open [editor.html](file:///c:/Users/USER/Downloads/vmcreatives/editor.html) in Chrome/Edge, drop the clips in, and click Export!)*
