## 🎬 Video Production Workflows

### 1. CapCut "Zero-Pro" Recipe (No Paid Paywall)
Every campaign includes a `CAPCUT_MASTER_RECIPE.md`. To avoid CapCut Pro fees:
* **Subtitles:** Import pre-formatted `scenes/subtitles.srt` instead of running CapCut's paid auto-caption tool.
* **Overlays:** Drag and drop transparent PNGs from `assets/badges/` onto the overlay track.
* **Sound Design:** Drop high-impact sound cues from `assets/sfx/` on beat markers.
* **Outro:** Splice `assets/outros/victorious_market_outro_1080p.mp4` directly at the end of the timeline.

### 2. Automated / Programmatic Video Editing (FFmpeg)
Use the automated pipelines in `tools/` to programmatically cut, composite, overlay badges, normalize audio to -14 LUFS, and render 1080p MP4 videos with zero manual timeline scrubbing.

### 3. The Reference & Deconstruction Lab (`references/`)
Whenever a competitor ad or viral TikTok video is discovered:
1. Save the reference video to `references/`.
2. Ask Antigravity AI to deconstruct its pacing, hook formula, audio cues, and typography.
3. Automatically generate matching asset packs and recipes adapted to Victorious Market.
