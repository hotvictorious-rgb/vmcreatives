* **Age:** 52 years old.
* **Build:** Slightly stocky, mature build, broad chest, upright and relaxed posture of a respected gentleman.

### 👔 Visual Identity & Signature Wardrobe
* **Complexion:** Warm, dark-brown Nigerian skin tone with natural maturity lines around eyes and forehead.
* **Face:** Broad mature face, strong facial structure, prominent cheekbones, rounded jaw, expressive dark-brown eyes, thick natural eyebrows, warm mischievous smile.
* **Hair & Beard:** Short, neatly trimmed black hair with noticeable grey strands around the temples. Well-maintained medium-short beard and moustache, predominantly black with natural grey hairs around the chin and sides.
* **Signature Outfit:** Beautifully tailored traditional Nigerian Senator-style suit in rich burgundy or wine color, featuring subtle traditional tonal embroidery along the neckline and chest placket, with matching trousers.
* **Footwear & Accessories:** Polished traditional leather shoes, simple classic wristwatch.
* **Strict Rule:** **No Victorious Market logo on his clothing.** He represents the traditional retail world and customer elder wisdom.

---

### 🧠 Personality, Humor & Social Dynamics
* **Personality:** Experienced, respected, observant, approachable, confident, and delightfully humorous. He has spent over 30 years in traditional markets and knows every trick of trading.
* **Humour Style:** Hearty, mature, teasing, and anecdotal. Chuckles at the impatience of young people, uses colorful local proverbs, and delivers sharp observational wisdom with a twinkle in his eye.
* **With Victorious:** Respects Victorious’s drive and modern e-commerce vision. Playfully questions new tech ("In my days, we touch the yam before we pay!"), but validates Victorious Market when he sees the depot pricing and genuine quality.
* **With Bassey:** Hilarious uncle-nephew dynamic. Teases Bassey whenever Bassey falls for fake online deals, shaking his head and saying, *"Bassey my boy, school fees you pay for market school!"*
* **With Eno:** Enjoys engaging with Eno’s sharp intellect and respects her ability to analyze quality.
