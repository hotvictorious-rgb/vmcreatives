# CHARACTER PROFILE: BASSEY (THE FUNNY EVERYDAY CUSTOMER)
## Master Visual Identity & Brand Specification

### 🎯 Confirmed Creative Direction
* **Character Name:** **BASSEY**
* **Role:** Victorious's close friend and permanent recurring comedy customer in the Victorious MARKET universe.
* **Age:** 29 years old.
* **Build:** Slightly stocky, average-height build, comfortable natural posture.
* **Nationality & Cultural Setting:** Nigerian (Uyo, Akwa Ibom State).
* **Physical Appearance:**
  - **Skin Tone:** Rich, deep medium-brown Nigerian skin tone.
  - **Face:** Naturally rounded face, full cheeks, broad forehead, highly distinctive and expressive facial features.
  - **Eyes & Eyebrows:** Lively, highly expressive dark eyes with thick, natural black eyebrows that move noticeably when surprised, confused, suspicious, or excited.
  - **Nose & Mouth:** Broad, slightly rounded nose, full natural lips, warm friendly smile, capable of producing hilarious expressions of shock, disappointment, confusion, and disbelief.
  - **Hair:** Short, neatly trimmed black hair in a clean low haircut with a subtle fade.
  - **Facial Hair:** Neatly maintained short black beard and moustache (slightly fuller than stubble, neat and well-groomed).

---

### 👔 Signature Clothing & Style Rule (Critical Distinction)

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        BASSEY'S SIGNATURE WARDROBE                     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Top: Casual Mustard-Yellow / Warm Golden-Yellow short-sleeved shirt   │
│  Trousers: Dark navy-blue or black casual trousers                     │
│  Shoes: Clean casual everyday sneakers                                 │
│  Rule: NO LOGOS ON HIS SHIRT (He is an everyday shopper, not staff)    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Shirt:** Casual mustard-yellow or warm golden-yellow short-sleeved shirt, comfortable fit (not tight, not oversized), unbuttoned top collar button.
* **Trousers:** Fitted dark navy-blue or black casual trousers.
* **Footwear:** Clean everyday casual sneakers.
* **Accessories:** Simple, practical wristwatch.
* **Crucial Rule:** **Never put the Victorious MARKET logo on Bassey's clothing.** Victorious wears the royal-purple branded polo; Bassey wears his own casual mustard-yellow shirt.

---

### 🎙️ Personality, Body Language & Comedy
* **Personality:** The everyday Nigerian customer—funny, dramatic, curious, slightly impatient, and hilariously suspicious when shopping online.
* **Body Language:** Naturally expressive. Talks with his hands, raises eyebrows when confused, opens eyes wide when shocked, dramatically puts hands on his head during disbelief.
* **Comedy Style:** Grounded in authentic Nigerian reactions, timing, and facial expressions—never cartoonish or ridiculous. Always likeable and deeply relatable to everyday shoppers.
* **Voice Cadence:** Fast, punchy, conversational Nigerian Pidgin with authentic Akwa Ibom/Uyo street flavor (*"Ah-ah! Wetin be this?!", "God abeg o!"*).
