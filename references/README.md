# Video Reference & Deconstruction Lab
## Victorious Market Creative Studio

This directory is the dedicated space for collecting and reverse-engineering high-performing reference videos (TikTok ads, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TV commercials, and viral skits).

---

### How The Workflow Works

1. **Drop Reference Media Here:**
   * Place reference video files (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`), audio tracks, or screenshots directly into this `references/` folder.
   * Example: `references/viral_skit_hook_01.mp4`, `references/jumia_flash_sale_style.mp4`.

2. **Request Breakdown:**
   * In chat, ask: *"Analyze `references/viral_skit_hook_01.mp4` and extract its editing style for Victorious Market."*

3. **What Gets Extracted & Generated:**
   * **Scene Cut Map:** Exact second-by-second pacing of hook, problem, proof, and call-to-action.
   * **Typography Blueprint:** Fonts, strokes, highlight colors, and subtitle animation timing.
   * **Sound Design Sheet:** Identifies every sound effect (riser, whoosh, boom, record scratch, meme stinger) and music ducking level.
   * **Asset Generation:** Recreates custom transparent badges (`assets/badges/`), custom SFX (`assets/sfx/`), and templates for CapCut.
   * **CapCut Recipe Card:** Step-by-step assembly instructions to reproduce the exact style with 100% free CapCut tools.
